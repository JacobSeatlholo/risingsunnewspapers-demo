A seat at the Christmas table: TAFTA launches annual campaign with support from special guest, Mariam Bassa

The Association for the Aged launches its annual Christmas Lunch Appeal, with support from South African actress Mariam Bassa.

RS By Rising Sun OnlineRising Sun Newspapers · 11 November 2026 · 2 min read

Demo layout: Real coverage, condensed: this sample page republishes the opening facts of the story published by Rising Sun Online on 11 November 2026, in the new article template. The full report lives on the live site.

The appeal

TAFTA (The Association for the Aged) has launched its annual Christmas Lunch Appeal, with support from South African actress Mariam Bassa, best known for her role as Aya Kandasamy.

During her visit, Bassa met residents and staff - photographed here with TAFTA resident Ivan Ronald - and spoke about what a festive gathering can mean for older persons: the chance to let their hair down, enjoy themselves and feel the warmth of the community around them.

Bassa also praised the dedication of TAFTA's staff, highlighting the care, humour and friendships that help create a welcoming environment for older persons.

How the community can help

Appeals like this one succeed because local people treat them as their own - donating festive groceries, sponsoring tables, volunteering on the day, and spreading the word through family WhatsApp groups and community pages.

The full details of this year's campaign, including how to donate, are in the original story on the live site.

The paper behind the campaign

Stories like this are why community newspapers exist. Nobody else spends the second week of November in a retirement home dining hall, taking down names and asking what Christmas means to the people seated there. The Rising Sun does - and has, for forty years.
