MEECA to hold candlelight ceremony in honour of Indian indentured labourers

The ceremony marks 166 years since the first Indian indentured labourers arrived in Natal aboard the SS Truro on 16 November 1860.

166 years since the SS Truro

The ceremony marks 166 years since the first Indian indentured labourers arrived in Natal aboard the SS Truro on 16 November 1860.

Members of MEECA gathered at the historic Mount Edgecombe Sugar Mill Chimney Site - a structure the organisation is seeking to adopt and preserve, so that future generations can learn about the men, women and children whose lives were shaped by indenture, and whose labour built the local sugar industry.

“Today, we stand on the shoulders of these courageous pioneers. Their hard work, perseverance, faith, and sacrifice laid the foundations upon which many families and communities…” - from MEECA's address, quoted in the original report

Why this story matters here

For forty years this paper has served the very communities whose forebears arrived on those ships. Reporting on commemorations like this one is not nostalgia journalism: it is the record of where those communities come from, kept in print and online for the generations who now read it on their phones.
