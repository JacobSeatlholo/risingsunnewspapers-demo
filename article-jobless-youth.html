Jobless youth question eThekwini's unemployment decrease

eThekwini's official unemployment rate fell from 27.8% to 22.2% between Q3 2025 and Q3 2026. Young residents are asking where those jobs actually are.

SS By Sibahle Anthony SiqathuleRising Sun Newspapers · 12 November 2026 · 4 min read

Demo layout: Real coverage, condensed: this sample page republishes the opening facts of the story reported for the Rising Sun by Sibahle Anthony Siqathule on 12 November 2026, in the new article template. The full report lives on the live site.

The numbers, and the reaction

The announcement that eThekwini's official unemployment rate dropped from 27.8% to 22.2% between the third quarter of 2025 and the third quarter of 2026 has been met with mixed reactions.

For some, the figures represent welcome progress. For others, they are a distant reflection of a reality characterised by unanswered job applications, financial hardship and qualifications that do not open doors.

That gap - between a percentage point on a slide and a payslip in a household - is exactly the ground a community newspaper covers: the ward meetings, the job queues, the learnership intakes, the young people asking what changed.
